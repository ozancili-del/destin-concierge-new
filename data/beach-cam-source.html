Understanding Destin Beach Flag Colors

Destin flies colored flags at all beach access points to indicate water conditions. Check the cams above to spot the flags before you head out:

🟢 Green
Calm conditions — low hazard. Safe for most swimmers.
🟡 Yellow
Moderate surf or currents — medium hazard. Use caution, especially with kids.
🔴 Red
High surf or strong currents — high hazard. Strong swimmers only.
🚩🚩 Double Red
Water closed to the public. No swimming — enforced by law. Fines apply.
🟣 Purple
Dangerous marine life — typically jellyfish or stingrays. Swim with caution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Destin have a live beach cam?

Yes — several live beach webcams stream from Destin and the Emerald Coast 24/7. The five cameras on this page cover Pelican Beach Resort, Crab Island, Okaloosa Island Pier, Sterling Sands, and the Beachhouse Webcam in Miramar Beach. All are truly live and update in real time.

What is the water temperature in Destin right now?

For today's actual Gulf water temperature, check the live conditions widget at the top of this page — it pulls real readings from NOAA updated every 6 minutes. Seasonally, water peaks at around 84°F in July and August. From October onward the Gulf cools quickly. December through March water is in the mid-50s — cold for swimming, but Pelican Beach Resort's indoor heated pool is open year-round. Check our full water temperature guide for month-by-month details.

How do I check if Destin beach is crowded today?

The live cams above are the best real-time check. In general, Destin is busiest late June through early August, especially on weekends between 10am and 4pm. For the least crowded experience arrive before 9am or after 4pm, and aim for weekdays. Spring break (mid-March through April) is the second busiest period.

What do the beach flag colors mean in Destin?

Green = calm and safe. Yellow = moderate surf, use caution. Red = high hazard, strong swimmers only. Double red = water closed, no swimming (enforced by law). Purple = dangerous marine life such as jellyfish or stingrays. Always check the flags when you arrive at the beach — they're posted at every beach access point.

What time is sunset in Destin?

Destin sunsets range from around 6pm in winter to 8:30pm in summer. Pelican Beach Resort faces west so the Pelican Beach cam above shows the exact sunset angle. The balconies of Unit 707 and Unit 1006 face directly into the sunset every evening.

Is the water in Destin really that clear and turquoise?

Yes — Destin's beach sand is 99% pure quartz crystal washed down from the Appalachian Mountains, which creates the vivid turquoise color and keeps the sand cool underfoot even in July. First-time visitors are consistently surprised by how clear the Gulf actually is. Check the cams on a sunny day and you'll see exactly what we mean.
